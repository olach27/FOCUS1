El maestro

"El Maestro" is a Tunisian drama series that tells the story of Hatem, a talented musician and former orchestra conductor who finds himself in an unexpected role at a juvenile rehabilitation center. Struggling to connect with the young residents, many of whom are victims of difficult life circumstances, Hatem uses music as a transformative tool to inspire hope and healing

Released: May 6, 2019

Genre:Drama,Inspirationl,Crime/Thriller

Casts:Lotfi Abdelli,Hend Sabry,Amine Gulse,Mouna Noureddin

Duration:45 min/episode

Country:Tunisia

Production: Cactus Prod (كاكتوس برود)
