Mr. Bean's Holiday

*Mr. Bean's Holiday* (2007) is a British comedy film starring Rowan Atkinson as the bumbling and hilarious Mr. Bean. In this sequel to *Bean* (1997), Mr. Bean wins a holiday trip to the French Riviera. However, things don't go as planned, and his chaotic adventure leads him to accidentally involve a young French boy, a film director, and the local police. The movie is filled with slapstick humor and showcases Mr. Bean’s unique ability to get into trouble, all while maintaining his childlike innocence and charm.

Released: 2007-03-30

Genre: Comedy, Family

Casts: Rowan Atkinson, Willem Dafoe, Emma de Caunes, Jean Rochefort

Duration: 90 min

Country: United Kingdom, France

Production: Working Title Films, StudioCanal
